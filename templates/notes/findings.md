# findings

One line each: what showed up and belongs neither to the ticket in hand nor to code this round changed. Nothing here is dispatched or labelled in the round it appeared, however urgent. At round close the Orchestrator marks each line `⇒ candidate` or `⇒ recommend discard`, and each defect with its origin, `origin: #N (ENG-x)` or `origin: gap`; the Definer reads this note at the start of every session and, with Jonatas, folds each marked line onto a ticket already defined and waiting for a manifest, makes it a ticket of its own, or discards it, and deletes the line then. A production emergency is not a finding: it is one line in "for you".
